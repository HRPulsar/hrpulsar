## Usage-Based Billing

Pay only for what you use. Every organization starts with a free tier — no credit card required.

- **Free tier** — 2,000 credits per month, automatically refilled. Unused credits never expire
- **Credit-based pricing** — each action (creating employees, running assessments, AI generation) has a transparent credit cost
- **Four credit packs** — Starter ($25 / 2,500 cr), Growth ($85 / 10,000 cr — most popular), Scale ($375 / 50,000 cr), Volume ($1,400 / 200,000 cr). Bigger packs unlock a better per-credit rate
- **Auto top-up (coming soon)** — set a balance threshold, pick a pack, and we refill automatically. No subscription, no expiring credits, cancel any time
- **Enterprise Annual contracts** — defined yearly budget at the steepest discount, with optional auto-renewal. Talk to sales
- **Read operations are always free** — browsing, viewing, and searching cost nothing
- **Spending visibility** — real-time credit balance, transaction history, and spending analytics
- **Bonus credits** — additional credits granted by support for special cases
- **Deduction order** — free credits are used first, then bonus, then purchased
- **No surprises** — cost confirmation before expensive operations
- **Long uploads reserve their credits** — starting an interview upload sets its cost aside for the length of the transfer, so a large recording cannot run out of balance halfway and fail at the end. The reservation is released the moment the upload finishes, is cancelled, or times out, and the balance page shows what is currently reserved
- **Top up from the dashboard** — request a credit pack right from Settings → Billing. Depending on the region we issue an invoice for bank transfer (credits land as soon as payment clears) or take you to online checkout
- **Local currency and invoicing** — regional and dedicated deployments price packs in the local currency and invoice from a local legal entity

## AI Competence Generation

Stand up a complete competency framework in minutes — or extend the one you already have without leaving the dashboard.

- **Four scopes** — generate the whole base, fill a single group, ask for indicators on one competence, or design the full competence matrix for a specialization (with grade-level distribution)
- **Specialization matrix from documents** — drop in a job description, KPI sheet, or onboarding plan (PDF, DOCX, XLSX, RTF, TXT) and the model proposes thematic competences, skill-level indicators, and a level-per-grade map ready to apply
- **Editable before commit** — every suggestion lands in a draft tree with cascade checkboxes; nothing reaches production until you press Apply
- **Refinement loop** — refine results in plain language ("add public-speaking", "drop legacy-MUI specifics", "rephrase for managers") before applying
- **Tenant-aware prompts** — your AI settings (preset, model, temperature, company description, language) flow into every run
- **Per-action credit cost shown up front** — the confirmation dialog lists what the run will cost so there are no surprises

## Position Planning with Competence Matrices

Treat specializations as first-class career profiles, not just dictionary entries — and connect every headcount line on the org chart to the competences that define it.

- **Specialization profiles** — each specialization gets its own page with grades, salary ranges, requirements, and a live count of positions and assigned employees per division
- **Competence matrix editor** — a `Competence × Grade` table per specialization with smart cascade rules: raise the bar on Junior and Middle, Senior automatically inherits; lower a Senior expectation and the lower grades follow
- **Position lifecycle and occupancy** — Active / On hold / Frozen / Closed status per role, with `Filled / Plan` drill-downs that show every employee on the line, flagged when their account, profile, assessments, or development plan need attention
- **Per-context learning materials** — keep one canonical material library and decide per (material × specialization) what to hide or surface, so a Backend developer's PDP doesn't compete with a Designer's reading list
- **Unassigned employees view** — an admin landing for HR to bulk-assign employees who came in without a position, no SQL or import dance required
